Equivalence Class Analysis

Following Kee, Johnson & Farid (2011): a class is the set of devices that share one signature (size n). Adding Huffman tables, dimensions, thumbnail and EXIF structure to the DQT should shrink classes and raise the share of uniquely identified devices.
